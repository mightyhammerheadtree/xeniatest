Why Xbox 360 games can't boot yet

A Xbox 360 game disc contains encrypted executables. Even the best desktop emulators need your console's NAND flash and CPU key to decrypt and boot them — xenia-canary included. Those files are copyrighted Microsoft material and are never shipped with this project; you must extract them from your own console.

On top of that, a playable web port still needs: the kernel (xboxkrnl) re-implemented against browser primitives, the Xenos GPU translated to WebGPU, AltiVec execution, FFmpeg/audio, and a threading story over SharedArrayBuffer. The roadmap lives in docs/PORTING_ROADMAP.md.
